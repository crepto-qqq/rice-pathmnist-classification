# Dataset and starter-code attribution

## Rice (Cammeo and Osmancik)

Dataset creators: **Ilkay Cinar and Murat Koklu**. Source: [UCI Machine Learning Repository](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/545/rice+cammeo+and+osmancik), [dataset DOI 10.24432/C5MW4Z](https://doi.org/10.24432/C5MW4Z). Licence: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Related publication requested by the dataset archive: Cinar, I. and Koklu, M. (2019), *Classification of Rice Varieties Using Artificial Intelligence Methods*, International Journal of Intelligent Systems and Applications in Engineering, 7(3), 188–194. [DOI 10.18201/ijisae.2019355381](https://doi.org/10.18201/ijisae.2019355381).

The retained `A1/clean_dataset/rice-final2.csv` is the supplied 1,400-row derivative subset of the original 3,810-row dataset. It has seven feature columns, 29 missing feature cells, and labels renamed to `class1` (600 Cammeo rows) and `class2` (800 Osmancik rows). Column spelling is preserved from the course CSV. Its feature values were compared against the official ARFF using a tolerance of 1e-5; all 1,400 rows matched on their nonmissing values. This establishes data provenance, not the exact procedure used to choose the subset or introduce missing values.

The numeric observations are credited to the original dataset creators. The notebook preprocessing, models and analysis are separate work. A second historical fixture, `test-before.csv`, has unverified provenance and is excluded throughout the imported history.

## PathMNIST / MedMNIST

PathMNIST is provided by the [MedMNIST project](https://medmnist.com/v2), which lists its dataset under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Reference: Yang et al., *MedMNIST v2 — A large-scale lightweight benchmark for 2D and 3D biomedical image classification*, Scientific Data (2023), [DOI 10.1038/s41597-022-01721-8](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-022-01721-8).

PathMNIST derives from the colorectal histology dataset described by Kather et al., *Predicting survival from colorectal cancer histology slides using deep learning: A retrospective multicenter study*, PLOS Medicine (2019), [DOI 10.1371/journal.pmed.1002730](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pmed.1002730).

The coursework array files are not distributed. Saved notebook sample-image visualisations are derived from this dataset; plots and confusion matrices were generated by the team's experiment code. The course selection differs from the official MedMNIST train/validation/test split, so the saved scores are not official benchmark results. Image reshaping, normalisation and course-specific splits are described in the notebooks. No endorsement by the dataset authors is implied.
